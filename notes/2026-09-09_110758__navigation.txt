October 25, 2024 | RESEARCH & PLANNING: LOAM LUXURY NAVIGATION

To balance *Loam Luxury*’s earthy, high-end editorial tone with raw digital craftsmanship, the navigation must abandon standard mega-menus in favor of tactile structural controls. Drawing from brutalism's exposure of site mechanics and Behance’s visual-first taxonomy, navigation should feel like opening a physical archivist’s drawer.

Concrete Implementation Angles:

* The "Strata" Structural Dock: Position a fixed, bottom-anchored menu bar using micro-scale technical type (`01 ARCHIVE`, `02 TAXONOMY`, `03 FIELD NOTES`, `04 DIRECTORY`). Hovering or tapping expands the menu upward in layered horizontal bands (resembling soil strata), revealing image-rich hover previews rather than plain text links.
* Raw Directory Overlay: Inspired by the raw, unadorned indexing of brutalist sites, replace the traditional hamburger menu with a fixed top-right coordinate trigger (`[DIR // 084_ITEMS]`). Activating it slides in a full-screen, high-contrast text grid listing every reviewed site categorized by typography, layout framework, and visual density. No nested sub-menus—just instant access to the entire index.
* Spatial Cursor Viewports: In text-heavy menu lists, hovering over a category or site title transforms the cursor into a floating, high-frame-rate visual thumbnail of the target page layout, giving the navigation a high-touch, museum-catalog feel.
* Scroll-Driven Breadcrumb Marquee: A sticky top bar that acts simultaneously as a scroll-depth progress meter and an interactive breadcrumb (`LOAM LUXURY > ISSUE 02 > EDITORIAL NAVIGATION > SITE_012`). Clicking any section label triggers a smooth horizontal pan to that specific content block, reinforcing the site's spatial layout.
