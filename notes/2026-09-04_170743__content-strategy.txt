May 18, 2024 — Content Strategy Note: Editorial Framework & Taxonomy

Observing the Framer Community Gallery highlights a critical gap in standard web design showcases: most platforms categorize by technology stack or template type rather than design intent and tactile quality. For *Loam Luxury*, our content strategy must elevate curation from a passive directory to an authoritative, editorial publication dissecting digital craft.

Key Content Pillars & Concrete Execution:

*   **Taxonomy of Digital Craft:** Replace generic tags ("Agency", "Portfolio", "Minimal") with curatorial attributes focused on feel and interaction philosophy: *Tactile Motion*, *Monolithic Typography*, *Spatial Pacing*, and *Non-Linear Storytelling*.
*   **The "Site Monolith" Breakdown:** Every featured site receives a structured 3-part teardown instead of a simple screenshot gallery:
    1. *The Core Thesis:* A two-sentence distillation of why the site’s execution matters to the industry.
    2. *Interaction Anatomy:* Frame-by-frame or video-annotated breakdowns of specific micro-interactions (e.g., cursor inertia, WebGL displacement shaders, page-transition timing).
    3. *The Spec Sheet:* High-density metadata detailing exact type pairings, performance metrics (FPS, page weight), and underlying interaction libraries.
*   **Micro-Critiques ("Field Notes"):** 75-word daily micro-features focusing on singular details—an unconventional navigation gesture, a subtle audio implementation, or a masterclass in grid breaking—offering quick, high-signal value.
*   **Curatorial Scarcity:** Cap featured sites at 3 per week to maintain a high bar of digital luxury. Balance this slow editorial rhythm with an unrefined, user-submitted "Raw Feed" where candidate sites undergo structured peer critique before being considered for a full editorial write-up.
