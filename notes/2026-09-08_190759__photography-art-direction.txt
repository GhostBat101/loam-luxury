2026-03-30 — Research & Art Direction Note: Photography Strategy for Loam Luxury

Visual Concept: "Tactile Digitalism"
To bridge the gap between digital web design and the organic grounding of the "Loam" brand, our art direction must reject sterile vector renders and flat screen captures. Drawing from recent Behance brand identity treatments that emphasize physical texture, we will treat web architecture as physical artifacts—photographing display panels embedded in raw basalt slabs, damp loam soil, and brushed dark copper frames under harsh, single-source key lighting.

Directional Execution & Key Assets:
* Extreme Macro UI Textures: Capture physical screen optics—magnified OLED subpixel arrays, light refraction through anti-glare glass, and printed cotton-paper wireframes showing ink bleeds. Use these as full-bleed ambient backgrounds for deep-dive site teardowns.
* The "Artifact" Display System: Moving past standard Landbook-style floating browser frames, establish a proprietary library of physical pedestals (cast concrete, scorched wood, dark terracotta). Case study sites will be photo-composited onto these real-world physical setups with matched directional lighting, natural drop shadows, and authentic reflections.
* Color & Mood Protocol: Dark-room studio lighting (chiaroscuro). Shadows are deep, warm charcoal (#12100E); highlights are sharp, cool 5600K neutral white. Accent lighting follows a subterranean spectrum: wet clay, oxidized silver, and deep peat brown.
* Editorial Guidelines for Featured Sites: Any creative site showcased on Loam Luxury must be presented with a dual-image pairing: 1) An ultra-sharp, uncompressed 1:1 viewport capture, paired with 2) A tactile environment photo shot at f/2.8 with shallow depth of field, focusing purely on a single physicalized micro-interaction or typographic detail.
