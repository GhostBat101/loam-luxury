HERO SECTION STRATEGY: TACTILE BRUTALISM & THE STRUCTURAL LENS

Drawing from Brutalist Websites' raw, unvarnished approach to screen real estate, Loam Luxury's hero section must abandon the standard editorial hero (centered high-contrast H1, full-bleed hero image, isolated pill CTA). For a site critiquing high-end digital design, the hero needs to feel like an architectural blueprint rather than a glossy billboard—pairing luxury typography with raw structural transparency.

Concrete Implementation Angles:

- Dual-Lens Mask Interaction: Divide the hero into a split view or interactive cursor mask. One layer renders a hyper-polished, luxury web component (e.g., fluid glassmorphism, serif typography), while the cursor reveals the raw underlying HTML/CSS grid structure and real-time layout metrics beneath. This establishes the site's core thesis—deconstructing creative web design—within the first three seconds.

- High-Density "Raw Index" Layout: Rather than a single headline, use a three-column asymmetric grid. Column 1: An oversized, variable-weight headline ("DECONSTRUCTING DIGITAL CRAFT") that shifts optical weight on mouse move. Column 2: A dense, monospaced live feed of latest site breakdowns tagged by mechanical metrics (`[DOM_DEPTH: 14]`, `[EASING: CUBIC-BEZIER]`). Column 3: A static vertical index acting as primary navigation.

- Tactile Color & Materiality: Merge raw web aesthetics with organic luxury. Use a stark, high-contrast palette of unbleached linen background (`#F4F1EA`), deep umber typography (`#1C1613`), and raw system-green accents for technical data tags. No smooth gradients or soft dropshadows; rely on hard 1px rule lines and visible grid borders to emphasize digital joinery.
