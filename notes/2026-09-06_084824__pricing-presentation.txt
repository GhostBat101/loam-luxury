March 30, 2026 — Research & Planning Note: Pricing Presentation for Loam Luxury

Standard SaaS pricing grids (three static cards with a "Most Popular" badge) directly contradict the high-fashion, editorial authority of Loam Luxury. Analysis of current Framer Gallery showcases demonstrates that the most effective creative agency/studio sites treat pricing as an interactive visual component rather than a utility table—using micro-interactions, high-contrast typographic hierarchy, and tactile state changes to signal premium value.

For Loam Luxury, we will execute a bespoke pricing architecture based on three concrete mechanics:

*   **The "Atelier Scope Sculptor" (Interactive Estimator):** Instead of static tiers, implement a modular, tactile scope builder. Clients select project parameters (e.g., *Bespoke WebGL Shaders*, *Editorial Direction*, *Custom Typography Architecture*, *Motion System*). As options toggle, a dynamic generative canvas behind the price display morphs in complexity alongside a real-time investment range (e.g., $25k – $80k+), elevating the scoping process into a design experience itself.
*   **Editorial Nomenclature & Framing:** Replace generic "Basic / Pro / Enterprise" labels with luxury commission phrasing: *Atelier Sprint* (focused launch), *Editorial System* (full digital brand architecture), and *Private Retainer* (ongoing creative direction). Each tier is presented with high-density serif typography, ultra-thin hairline borders, and zero clutter—framing the cost as a capital investment in digital art direction rather than billable hours.
*   **Transparent Deliverable Accordions with "No-RFP" Friction:** High-end clients require absolute clarity without cheap sales friction. Beneath each tier, use smooth expandable panels detailing exact output guarantees (e.g., 60fps animation budget, full source code handover, zero-dependency builds). Pair this with an explicit "Inquiry Gate" requiring a minimum project floor, subtly filtering out low-budget RFPs before the intake form triggers.
