March 29, 2026 — Visual Identity & Brand Direction

* Concept Core: "Elevated Earth & Exposed Wireframes." Merging the unpolished, structural honesty seen in brutalist web archives with high-craft editorial publishing. The visual identity must avoid the sterile, smoothed-out "SaaS aesthetic" by deliberately exposing the scaffolding of web design through an earthy, tactile lens.

* Typographic Contrast System:
  - Primary Brand Mark & Headlines: A high-contrast, razor-sharp display serif with exaggerated ink traps (e.g., modified GT Sectra or custom display serif) to embody "Luxury."
  - Meta/UI Annotations: Raw, unstyled monospaced type (`ui-monospace`, native system stack) rendered in all-caps with explicit character spacing. Used for site metadata, inspect-element style tags, and visual callouts ("SITE ID: 089", "VIEWFRAME: 1440px") to continually reinforce the "site about creative sites" theme.

* Texture & Layout Artifacts (The "Loam" Layer):
  - Ditch flat background solids for a persistent, subtle noise grain shader layered over deep Umber (#1C1A17) and Chalk (#F4F1EA).
  - Use exposed, structural 1px gridlines with visible coordinate nodes at layout intersections instead of invisible padding. 
  - Section dividers mimic physical soil strata or structural schematics, blending organic curves with technical CAD-style dimension markers.

* Visual Treatment for Showcased Work:
  - To prevent featured sites from clashing with the Loam identity, default showcase thumbnails process through an automated 2-bit dither or heavy monochrome halftone effect.
  - Hover/focus states trigger an fluid "unearthing" transition: the dither mask dissolves, revealing the showcased site's full high-fidelity, high-color capture.

* Brand Mark & Identity Assets:
  - Primary Glyph: A stylized topographical cross-section enclosed within a standard 16:9 viewport frame—symbolizing the intersection of natural depth ("loam") and digital container ("luxury web").
  - Stamp/Badge System: Circular, rotating SVG text seals ("CURATED ARCHIVE • LOAM LUXURY • EST 2026") stamped directly across section breaks, mimicking physical editorial proofing seals.
